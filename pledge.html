The Kids-First Pledge

Every line below is checkable. If we ever break one, hold us to it.

  1. Points go up. Never down. Nothing your child earns can be taken away — not points, not progress, not a pet behind a paywall.
  2. Your child will never be told to ask you for money. No ads, no coins, no memberships to beg for. Prices are only ever shown behind the parent gate.
  3. One price, once — and you choose it. Three prices, same app. Pay less if money is tight, pay more to cover a family who can’t. No subscription, ever: we don’t rent math to children.
  4. There is nothing to cancel — but if there were, it would take two taps.
  5. No child locked out. If the price is hard right now, email us — it’s free, no questions asked. hello@smallstepsmath.app
  6. Your child’s data never leaves your iPad. The privacy label says “Data Not Collected” because none is. Read the whole policy — it’s short.
  7. Stuck never means “look at the example.” Stuck means a smaller step.
  8. The method — and its limits — published in full. We never say “proven.” We say measured, on your own dashboard.
